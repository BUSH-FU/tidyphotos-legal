What TidyPhotos is

TidyPhotos is a private, on-device iOS app that helps you clean up your photo and video library through a fast, swipe-based flow. Swipe up to mark for deletion, swipe down to keep, double-tap to favorite. You can also drag photos into custom albums and attach short voice memos to the moments that matter.

Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not connect to any server operated by us or anyone else. There is no account, no login, and no analytics. Your photos, videos, voice memos, in-app progress, and preferences live exclusively on your device.

Trust & Privacy

Privacy promise. TidyPhotos is built around a single rule: your photos never leave your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or analyze your photos, videos, voice memos, or any other personal information. No third-party SDK in the app has access to your media library or your voice memos.
